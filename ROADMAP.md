# Roadmap

Milestone-level, deliberately undated. Skyperp is built phase by phase, and each phase has to earn the next.

### ✅ Validation
Prove the idea is real before building. Backtest the underlying for tradability, model the protocol economics under adversarial conditions, map the competitive and regulatory landscape, and commit to kill criteria up front. **Done:** the underlying is tradable and the economics hold under stress.

### ✅ Protocol design
Architect the system: oracle pipeline, three-price model, margin and funding mechanics, liquidation engine, failure-mode playbooks. Every major decision logged. **Done.**

### 🔜 Proof of concept (Solana devnet)
A live, working market on devnet. Real oracle ingest, an orderbook, funding, liquidations. Small and self-funded. The goal is not revenue, it is "people traded it and it held."

### Demand validation (in parallel)
The thing that actually gates everything: do traders and hedgers want to size real positions on this? Direct conversations, not assumptions.

### Mainnet launch
Audited programs, conservative position limits, a single proven market (NYC weather). Grow caps as liquidity proves itself.

### Expansion
The substrate generalizes. Once the weather wedge works, the same construction extends to other continuous real-world indices: more cities, then other weather variables, then beyond weather entirely.
