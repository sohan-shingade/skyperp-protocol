How It Works

Technical Deep Dive

This section is for people who want to understand the mechanism. If you trade perps, build protocols, or evaluate derivatives systems, this is the substance.

The Oracle: Three Separate Prices

A naive approach blends external data with on-exchange trading into one composite oracle. That's circular -- the exchange's own trading feeds back into the oracle that determines PnL. Skyperp uses three completely independent prices:

Oracle Index

100% external. Trimmed mean of three independent government agency forecasts (NOAA, ECMWF, DWD). Cannot be manipulated via on-exchange activity.

Used for: PnL, liquidation triggers, liquidation execution

Mark Price

2-hour EMA of orderbook trades, hard-clamped to oracle ± max deviation. Can drift, but can't escape oracle gravity.

Used for: Funding rate calculation only

Trade Price

Phoenix CLOB fill price. Whatever the orderbook gives you at execution.

Used for: Entry/exit price, realized PnL

Why this matters: If someone manipulates orderbook trading, they can only affect funding rates (which are bounded by caps). They cannot affect PnL, trigger liquidations, or corrupt the oracle. The oracle is externally anchored.

Funding Rate: How Carry Replaces Settlement

In traditional perps, funding pulls the mark toward spot. Skyperp has no spot market, so funding pulls the mark toward the external oracle instead.

Standard funding formulas divide by price, which breaks when the underlying crosses zero. This index crosses zero regularly. Skyperp uses absolute divergence:

funding_rate = clamp(K × (mark - oracle) × multiplier, -max_rate, +max_rate)

K         = 0.1% per °F per hour
max_rate  = 0.5% per hour
multiplier = 1x (normal) / 2x (2-4°F divergence) / 3x (>4°F divergence)

A Known Tradeoff: Funding vs. Price Discovery

Acknowledged, not hidden: Traders who correctly anticipate the next oracle update pay funding for being right. There's no "spot" to arbitrage against (unlike BTC perps). This is inherent to any instrument on a non-tradable index. The carry cost of anticipation is bounded (0.5%/hr max), and we believe this is acceptable for the same reason prediction markets work -- the carry cost IS the price of expressing a view before consensus updates.

System Architecture

Solana-native. Phoenix orderbook for matching. Five onchain programs, five offchain services.

How a Trade Executes

1
Submit order via frontend or API. Market program validates: market active, margin sufficient, within position limits.
2
Phoenix CLOB matches against resting liquidity. Returns fill price.
3
Position created with entry = fill price. Margin locked. Funding index snapshot stored for lazy settlement.
4
PnL accrues continuously based on oracle index (external). Funding accrues based on mark-oracle divergence.
5
Close anytime. Exit at CLOB fill. Accumulated funding settles. Margin released.

How Oracle Updates Work

1
NWP model publishes new forecast (4x daily). Oracle ingest service fetches, computes anomaly per model.
2
Onchain update writes per-source values. Enforces: sanity bounds (±20°F), monotonic timestamps (no replays), durable nonce prohibition (no held-back stale submissions).
3
Trimmed mean recomputed (median of 3). Mark re-clamped to new oracle bounds. Staleness status updated.

The System Is Manipulation-Resistant

The oracle is built entirely from external, government-published weather feeds, so orderbook activity cannot move the reference price, only the funding signal. An attacker pays quadratic slippage cost for a bounded, linear payoff, and position limits cap the prize. Honest Risks

What could kill this, in order of severity.

1

Demand Uncertainty

Novel instrument. 53 potential users identified, conversations in progress.

But: Ventuals proved non-tradable index perps find traders ($215M in 4 months). The PoC is designed to test this cheaply before committing capital.

2

No Spot Arbitrage

Unlike BTC perps, no spot market to hedge against. Funding convergence relies on mean-reversion traders, not arbitrageurs.

But: 100% convergence across 36 parameter combos in simulation. Prediction markets also lack spot and still converge. The funding mechanism is the arbitrage.

3

Liquidity Cold Start

Protocol market maker solves mechanics. Doesn't solve demand.

But: Hyperliquid bootstrapped with points and 4K organic users at $0 marketing. Protocol MM provides the baseline; crypto Twitter + novel underlying drives discovery.

4

Copyable Mechanism

Public data, documented design. Anyone could replicate.

But: Execution speed matters more than mechanism secrecy. Calibration learning (oracle tuning, MM parameters) compounds over time. Drift and dYdX coexist with identical perp mechanics.

5

Regulatory Uncertainty

CFTC posture could change. Offshore + geofencing is standard but not guaranteed.

But: Weather = excluded commodity (26yr CME precedent). CFTC actively enabling onchain perps (Coinbase Jul 2025). US DCM path opening. Legal memo budgeted.
